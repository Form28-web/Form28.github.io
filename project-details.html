Audio Bible Player

The first four phases of this project have now been completed, and we are preparing to move into the next stage: Prototype 2. The project began with ideation, where an initial vision was developed into a technically grounded concept. From the beginning, the core goal has remained consistent: to create a distraction-free audio Bible that is intuitive to use regardless of technological background, allowing users to quickly navigate between books, chapters, and eventually individual verses.

The Proof-of-Concept 1 and Proof-of-Concept 2 stages allowed us to progressively validate the core functionality and technical architecture of the device. Through these phases, we developed and tested the navigation system, user interface, audio playback approach, display, controls, storage, and other major electronic systems. These early prototypes challenged several aspects of the original concept, helped identify better solutions, and ultimately provided a strong technical foundation for the first complete prototype.

Prototype 1 has now been completed. This phase brought the major hardware and software systems together into a functional handheld device for the first time. The prototype successfully demonstrated the core user experience, including rapid navigation through all 66 books of the Bible, chapter selection, audio playback, physical controls, volume adjustment, playback seeking, and a simple on-screen interface. Just as importantly, Prototype 1 allowed us to evaluate how the electronics, software, controls, audio system, and physical enclosure perform together as a complete device.

We are now looking ahead to Prototype 2. This next stage will build on everything learned through Prototype 1 with a greater focus on refinement, reliability, and moving the design closer to something that could eventually be manufactured. Development will include improvements to the electronics, firmware, mechanical design, user experience, and overall system integration. We will also continue exploring more advanced functionality, including precise verse navigation and bookmarking, while developing a clearer path toward purpose-built hardware and future production.

For supporters and volunteers, this remains an exciting time to get involved with the Audio Bible Player project. The project has progressed from an idea, through multiple proof-of-concept systems, and now through its first complete functional prototype. There is still meaningful engineering and development work ahead as we refine the device and prepare for the next stage. Whether your background is in electronics, mechanical design, software, manufacturing, or you have a desire to contribute financially, there are opportunities to help us develop a reliable tool that we pray the Lord will use to help people engage with His Word in a practical and effective way.
