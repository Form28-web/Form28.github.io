Volunteer Opportunities:

Electronics Engineer

ForM28 is looking for an Electronics Engineer to help us on our Audio Bible Player project. This individual will contribute to hardware refinement, circuit design, prototyping, and validation as we move from the completed Prototype 1 into future prototypes and early versions of the device. We are looking for someone who can provide experienced insight into sound electronics development practices, helping us improve system reliability, refine the hardware architecture, and begin preparing the design for the transition from prototype electronics toward a more integrated and production-ready system.

Software Engineer

ForM28 is looking for a Software Engineer to help us on our Audio Bible Player project. This individual will contribute to firmware development and refinement as we move from the completed Prototype 1 into future prototypes and early versions of the device. We are looking for someone with experience in embedded software who can provide insight into firmware architecture, reliability, and development practices while helping us refine the user experience and build the software foundation for future features such as precise verse navigation, bookmarking, and improved audio playback.
